# Personal Design Portfolio
CECS-101: Design Your Degree, Design Your Career College of Emerging and Collaborative Studies, University of Tennessee

A record of how I'm designing my academic and professional path, built across the course using the Design Thinking framework: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test.

##About Me

I'm Stephen Matthews, an Applied AI student at the University of Tennessee. Before tech, I grew up on a farm, served in the Marine Corps, and danced professionally. My technical background is self-taught, and includes idle and tower defense games, a self-hosted and hardened server, websites, and databases.

##About This Portfolio

This is a record of thinking rather than a highlight reel. Each module adds a design journal entry documenting what I discovered, what I decided, and how my plan changed along the way. Alongside the journals, this portfolio collects the self-assessments, reflections, and planning documents that feed into my Career-Degree Roadmap, which is the final deliverable of the course.

##Academic and Career Goals

I'm working toward a degree in Applied AI focused on machine learning and computer vision. I want to build software and games, and eventually an application that teaches dance at home, using computer vision to correct technique and timing. Dance instruction has a real access problem, since it depends on someone watching you and telling you what's wrong. That's a delivery problem, and delivery problems are solvable with technology.
